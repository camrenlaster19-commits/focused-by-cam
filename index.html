  • Clients are welcome to reschedule with at least 24 hours’ notice.

Please Read

Booking Policies

Retainers and Payments

A nonrefundable retainer is required to reserve your appointment. The remaining balance is due no later than the beginning of the session.

No Refunds

All retainers and payments are nonrefundable. Clients may reschedule according to the booking policy and transfer the amount paid one time to another available date.

Rescheduling

Rescheduling requests should be made at least 24 hours before the appointment. Payments may be transferred one time to a new available date.

Priority Turnaround

Priority / Same-Day Turnaround is available for +$45 on eligible sessions and is subject to availability.

Edited Images

Sessions include all best images professionally edited. Focused by Cam selects the strongest usable photographs; RAW files, test shots, duplicates, and unfinished images are not included.

Turnaround

Standard turnaround is 36–72 hours. Larger events, commercial projects, or unusually large galleries may require additional delivery time.
