Before You Book

Booking Details

  • A nonrefundable retainer is required to reserve your session.
  • The remaining balance is due on or before the day of your session.
  • Clients are welcome to reschedule with at least 24 hours’ notice.
  • Same-day appointments are subject to availability and include a $25 fee.
  • You will receive a private proof gallery to choose your final edits.
  • Standard turnaround is 2–3 business days after selections are submitted.

Please Read

Booking Policies

Retainers and Payments

A nonrefundable retainer is required to reserve your appointment. The remaining balance is due no later than the beginning of the session.

No Refunds

All retainers and payments are nonrefundable. Clients are always welcome to reschedule and transfer the amount paid to another available date.

Rescheduling

Rescheduling requests should be made at least 24 hours before the appointment. Payments may be transferred one time to a new available date.

Same-Day Bookings

Same-day appointments include an additional $25 fee and are subject to availability and approval.

Proofs and Edits

Clients receive a private proof gallery and select the photographs included with their package for professional editing.

Turnaround

Final edited photographs are delivered within 2–3 business days after image selections are submitted.
